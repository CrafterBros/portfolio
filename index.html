Featured Project

Pet Chests & Survival Essentials

Over 6 months of development · Completed · Future updates planned

Pet Chests & Survival Essentials is a Minecraft Bedrock add-on designed to make survival gameplay more enjoyable and convenient without removing the feeling of playing survival Minecraft. The add-on combines useful quality-of-life systems with living companions that can store the player's items and provide different gameplay effects.

Pet Chests

Five different living chest companions, each providing different gameplay effects and functionality.

Spawner Silk Touch

Allows mob spawners to be collected as blocks using a Silk Touch pickaxe.

Tree Feller

Breaking a connected tree log automatically cuts down the rest of the tree.

Vein Miner

Mining an ore automatically mines the connected ore vein.

Mob Health Bar

Displays a mob's name and health bar in the action bar when looking at it.

Stack Refill

Automatically replaces a depleted held item stack with another available stack from the inventory.

Anvil Repair

Provides a gradual repair system using iron ingots while crouching and interacting with the item.

Gravestone

Stores the player's items at the location where they died.

Craftable Mob Spawn Eggs

Allows mob spawn eggs to be crafted using custom materials and mob egg shells.

Horse Armor Recipes

Adds crafting recipes for iron, gold and diamond horse armor.

Encumbering Stone

Prevents item pickup while held in the inventory and can be toggled through interaction.
